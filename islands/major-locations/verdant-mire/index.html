Verdant Mire

Type:Island
Level:2,200 - 3,500

Description

Verdant Mire is a hostile, toxic swamp designed as a pure, high-level grinding zone.

With a focus on intense combat and repeatable quests, it serves as a straightforward gauntlet for players to level up and prepare for the next major island.
